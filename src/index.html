About the Artist

Capturing truth
through the lens

I'm Marwan — a 20-year-old photographer and video editor from Alexandria, Egypt. With over six years behind the lens and currently studying Computer Science at the Arab Open University, I sit at the intersection of technology and art. Every frame I make is a deliberate act — a negotiation between light, shadow, and the decisive moment.

My work spans wildlife photography, portraiture, and editorial work — always guided by the belief that photography is not just documentation, but transformation. I also work with Adobe Premiere Pro, Photoshop, and Lightroom to bring stories to life in post. So far I have worked mostly with family and friends, on photo shoots and on photo and video edits, and I am now opening up to new projects.
